    How to play

    Crack the secret five-letter word in six tries. Each letter sits on its own dial.

    Type any real word and press Enter. Every dial then plays higher or lower:

    ALocked. Right letter. It stays set for you. C▲Higher. The right letter comes later in the alphabet, towards Z. N▼Lower. The right letter comes earlier, towards A.

    So if the code is STACK and you try CRANE:

    C▲R▲AN▼E▲

    Under each dial you'll see the letters it could still be, like D–Z. To change a letter, tap its dial and type a new one.

    The self-destruct clock gives you three minutes, starting when you submit your first word. Stuck? Tap Hint next to the clock to crack one dial, but it costs 30 seconds.

    Everyone gets the same code. A new one arrives every day at 7am UK time.
